TF-Tracker - Privacy Policy

TF-Tracker ("Tracking of Transformer") is a web app that helps electricity distribution offices track transformer complaints, PRs, issue and replacement. Last updated: 16 October 2026.

What the app does with your Google account

Only a Division Head (Admin) setting up the app is asked to sign in with Google, once. The app asks for access to Google Drive and your email address, and uses it only to:

  • change the sharing of the one Google Sheet that you paste into the app, so that the app can read and write it;
  • read your email address, to show who is setting up the app and to keep a registry of Admins.

The access token stays in your browser's memory, is used once, and is revoked right after setup. The app does not read, copy, change or delete any other file in your Google Drive. Staff and users who only log in with an ID and password never sign in with Google.

What data is stored

  • Your Google Sheet: all transformer records (complaint numbers, PR numbers, places, JE and driver names and mobile numbers, dates) and user IDs with password hashes are stored in the Google Sheet owned by the Admin, in the Admin's own Google account.
  • Registry: the developer keeps a registry (a Google Sheet) with the Admin's email address, Profit Center ID, DISCOM, workshop names and the link of the Admin's sheet, so that users can log in and so that complaint status can be searched by DISCOM.
  • The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Your browser stores only small preferences (for example the DISCOM you selected).

Complaint status page

Anyone can check the status of a complaint on the home page by selecting a DISCOM and typing a complaint or PR number. The result shows the complaint details stored by that DISCOM's offices, including the JE and driver name and mobile number.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties, except as needed to run the app on Google (Google Sheets, Google Apps Script) and GitHub Pages.

Your choices

  • You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can delete your Google Sheet or stop sharing it at any time; the app then no longer has access to the data.
  • To ask for your entry to be removed from the registry, contact the developer using the developer contact email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
